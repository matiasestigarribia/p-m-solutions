01 — Typography

Full type scale

Primary typeface: Geist. Monospace: Geist Mono (code, labels, metadata). Headings use weight 500 with tight -0.02em tracking; large headings apply a white → zinc vertical gradient. Body text is 16px at 1.7 line-height.

05 — Motion & Interaction

Entrances use a signature cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) ease over 0.9–1s. Ambient loops (typing cursor, terminal reveal, floating logos, equalizer bars) run continuously to give the interface a "live system" feel.
